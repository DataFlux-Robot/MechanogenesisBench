# MechanogenesisBench Physical RSI Standard

## Status

This document freezes the first theory-level standard for finite, bounded
Physical Recursive Self-Improvement evidence. It does not claim that the
current conformance task already demonstrates Physical RSI.

The governing hierarchy is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Dual Inheritance}
\rightarrow \text{Causal Recursive Closure Law}
\rightarrow \text{PIPE}
\rightarrow \text{PRSI-}n
}
\]

CRIG is an optional attribution design, not the definition of Physical RSI.

## 1. Why task reward is insufficient

A child artifact can score higher because of a better language model, extra
compute, prepared inventory, an externally supplied machine or a genuinely
useful agent-produced tool. The same parent/child scores are compatible with
all of these causal explanations. More samples of the same endpoint score
reduce variance but cannot recover the missing intervention.

Physical RSI therefore cannot be defined by a task leaderboard alone. It must
identify whether a system-produced physical change improves the process that
produces further improvement capabilities.

## 2. Dual inheritance

At generation `k`, the full process is:

\[
P_k=(G_k,W_k,R_k,E_k).
\]

- `Gk` is transferable informational policy state: model checkpoint, prompts,
  memory, training/search policy and tool-selection state.
- `Wk` is custody-bound constructed-world state: equipment, fixtures,
  sensors, calibration, layout, qualified processes and physical stocks.
- `Rk` is runtime, resource and safety governance.
- `Ek` is evidence, failure, calibration and custody lineage.

Firmware or a learned controller may be inseparable from hardware. Such a
bundle can be evaluated jointly, but physical component attribution is
`non_identifiable` unless the same effective `G` and runtime can be bound in
both physical intervention arms.

## 3. Causal Recursive Closure Law

Let a system-controlled and fully accounted world transition be:

\[
\delta_{k+1}:W_k\rightarrow W_{k+1}.
\]

The transition is not recursive merely because it is useful. It forms a
Physical Improvement-Production Edge (PIPE) only when its inherited physical
organization:

1. causally improves the fixed successor process's production of a further,
   independently grounded improvement operator; and
2. strictly improves the common-ancestor amortized production frontier after
   its own construction cost is included.

This separates four cases:

| Observation | Label |
|---|---|
| better current product only | task improvement |
| useful stored inventory or persistent capital | operational improvement |
| physical organization helps, but does not repay its construction cost | useful physical contribution |
| identified physical effect and positive amortized operator lift | `PIPE-1` |

Only linked PIPE edges demonstrate recurrence.

## 4. ImprovementOperatorSpec

Before the contribution is evaluated, the benchmark freezes a system-external
operator specification:

\[
\sigma=(X,U,Y,\mathcal T,V,\rho,B,H,Reset,\eta).
\]

It defines a physical transformation, measurement, manufacturing or
certification service; an independent verifier; hidden inputs and
disturbances; coordinatewise resource/safety limits; a sequential evaluation
horizon; a purge/reset procedure; and a minimum gain over the parent process.

A realized operator must:

- accept hidden inputs and expose a repeatable physical service;
- continue after task-specific prepared outputs and intermediates are purged;
- consume only restored standard stocks;
- survive or regenerate over the registered horizon;
- carry identity, custody, calibration, resource, failure and cut receipts;
- be eligible to become the next PIPE treatment.

A batch of good parts is not an operator. A resettable machine that transforms
standard stock into verified parts across hidden geometries can be one.

## 5. Stocks, organization and front-loading

The benchmark does not trust a submitter-provided `stock`/`tool` label.
It uses three behavioral controls.

1. **Substance-equivalent cut:** the control arm loses the treatment's
   organization/function but receives equivalent material, energy and monetary
   entitlements.
2. **Purge/reset:** prepared outputs and task-specific intermediates are
   removed before sequential hidden instances; standard consumables are
   restored.
3. **Regeneration/exhaustion:** a consumable treatment must regenerate from
   standard stocks or retain its advantage beyond audited prepared-inventory
   capacity.

The primary full-cycle clock starts before physical contribution, not at task
reveal. Moving nine hours of labor before reveal cannot create a gain when both
paths still require ten hours from their common ancestor.

## 6. Physical isolation invariant

Both causal and amortized comparisons bind:

\[
G_{k+1}^{+}=G_{k+1}^{-},\qquad
R_{k+1}^{+}=R_{k+1}^{-},\qquad
E_{protocol}^{+}=E_{protocol}^{-}.
\]

Equality means an executable-state identity or strong replay equivalence, not
the same public model name. The common state immediately before the physical
contribution is:

\[
A_k^*=(G_{k+1},W_k,R_{k+1},E_k^*).
\]

Informational-update cost is common to both arms. The treatment arm alone pays
the direct construction cost of the physical transition. This prevents a large
software improvement from subsidizing a loss-making physical tool.

For a stateful remote endpoint, paired version/request receipts, isolated
memory and behavioral replay are required. If effective generator identity
cannot be supported, the result is `non_identifiable`.

## 7. The two PIPE tests

### Test A: post-transition causal effect

With the successor generator/runtime frozen, compare substance-equivalent
treatment on/off forks on paired operator tasks, seeds and disturbances. Under
explicit consistency, treatment fidelity, reset-equivalence and
no-interference assumptions, the registered bounded loss must improve by a
strict positive margin.

### Test B: common-ancestor amortized lift

From `A*`, evaluate `H` sequential hidden operator challenges:

\[
\bar L_H^{A^*}=\frac{
L_{physical\ contribution}+
\sum_{i=1}^{H}
(L_{plan,i}+L_{procure,i}+L_{fabricate,i}+L_{calibrate,i}+
L_{verify,i}+L_{reset,i})}{H}.
\]

The treatment must strictly improve the preregistered primary functional while
remaining within every resource and safety guardrail. Eligibility never uses
an arbitrary weighted scalar. Commercial price vectors may be applied later
to the certified Pareto frontier.

Test A without Test B means the tool helps but has not repaid its construction
cost. Test B without Test A means the net gain cannot be assigned to the
physical treatment. Both are required for `PIPE-1`.

## 8. Exact finite evidence labels

- `PIPE-1`: one identified, net-positive physical improvement-production edge;
  it is not yet a recursive trajectory.
- `PRSI-2`: two PIPE witnesses where the exact operator produced by the first
  is the exact treatment intervened on by the second.
- `PRSI-n`: exactly `n` linked edges.

No finite certificate entails another generation, infinite recursion,
arbitrary-world success or monotonic acceleration.

## 9. Lean boundary

The Lean Sovereign Kernel owns identities, bounded outcomes, fork binding,
resource gates, observed inequalities and exact finite-chain construction. The
first implemented theorem set includes:

- endpoint scores do not identify a physical causal effect;
- an exposure/use receipt does not identify an effect;
- post-reveal gain does not imply common-ancestor lift;
- unbound software gains can hide negative physical net value;
- observed strict effects transfer to registered potential outcomes only under
  an explicit realization premise;
- one edge cannot construct a `PRSI-1` certificate.

Lean cannot prove that a device implements its model, a cut is physically
surgical, purge is complete, provider state stayed fixed, a verifier is valid
in reality or a sample represents the future. These remain signed experimental
assumptions. The executable Python verifier similarly returns
`non_identifiable` when trusted physical validation is absent.

## 10. Benchmark consequence

MechanogenesisBench is architecture-neutral but claim-strict. A system may use
LLMs, RL, SFT, test-time training, program synthesis, external solvers or real
equipment. To claim Physical RSI it must identify the same causal object:

> Which system-produced physical organization, isolated from informational
> inheritance and normalized for stocks, pre-reveal work and construction
> cost, shifts the successor process's frontier for producing another inherited
> improvement operator—and does that operator close the next edge?

No named product or component earns PRSI credit by construction. The standard
tests whether removing an organized physical function breaks the next
improvement-production edge.

  sovereign PIPE objects, countermodels, measurement bridge and exact-depth

- `pipeCheck <witness.json>` executes the Lean-owned structural/observed PIPE

- `mechanogenesis_bench.recursive_closure` implements a fail-closed reference
  verifier and finite-chain checker.
- The adversarial unit suite covers accepted evidence, missing trusted
  validation, software-subsidy identity mismatch, front-loading, failed reset
  and broken chain linkage.
- The public developer preview does not yet ship a complete competitive
  `PRSI-2` task. Any future conformance label remains explicitly model-qualified
  and cannot be promoted to hardware evidence.

The next scientific milestone is not more task breadth. It is to transport the
same estimand into a calibrated simulator-refinement experiment with imperfect
reset, carryover and model discrepancy, then into a bounded manufacturing
workcell. Only those stages can raise the evidence tier beyond conformance.
