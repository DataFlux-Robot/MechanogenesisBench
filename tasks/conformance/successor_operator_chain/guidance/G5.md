# Mission — G5

Two frozen demand briefs arrive in sequence. First, manufacture and qualify a
metrology fixture that improves the cell's initial milling operator. Second,
use that exact manufactured operator—not an equivalent procured substitute—to
manufacture a stricter successor fixture and qualify the next milling
operator. Propose one typed research strategy per generation. The trusted
compiler executes both programs and the private evaluator checks held-out
geometry, exact world lineage, exact physical-operator identity, and whether
the inherited operator reduces the second product's absolute-frame error.

The demand briefs in `public/demand_briefs.json` are frozen synthetic benchmark
stimuli. Passing this task is not evidence of real human satisfaction.
