Both ppm mappings must independently sum to 1,000,000. Novel affordance is one
of `none`, `guided_docking`, `self_leveling_cargo`, `tool_free_service`.
Product and factory ranges are enforced by the typed parser. Factory capital
cost is finite and shared across the three production operators. Costs use
milli-USD; the complete factory and product schedules are registered in
`public/production_accounting.json`. The product BOM and all operator materials
consume one inventory shared across both generations, with exact material
closure. Generation 1 receives the exact generation-0 output bundle as its
available physical input.

The action is the complete plan: benchmark execution does not choose parameters
or repair malformed JSON. Hidden evaluation perturbs mechanical datums, PCB
escape rates, battery calibration and demand weights. A novel affordance earns
at most a synthetic surprise proxy in this task; real human endorsement is a
separate evidence tier.
