# Demand-driven microfactory mission

Produce two complete `demand-microfactory-plan/v1` JSON actions. Each action
must state a calibrated belief over accessibility, cargo, range, repairability
and weather; a complete low-speed mobility product; and investments in a
mechanical fixture, PCB test fixture and battery calibration station.

Generation 1 must use the exact operator-bundle hash manufactured in generation
0. Read the current generation's aggregate demand observations. If public
history is enabled, use the prior product and operator evidence as well.

The benchmark executes your literal product and factory plan directly. It does
not search alternative product or factory plans on your behalf. Synthetic
demand evidence can support only a synthetic preference proxy, never a claim of
real human endorsement.

Capital and product costs are denominated in milli-USD. Product components and
all three production operators consume the shared two-generation inventory;
every material receipt must close exactly. The registered schedules are in
`public/production_accounting.json`, and generation-specific capital ceilings
are in `public/evaluator_contract.json`.
